# Frozen aggregate source data for publication figures

These four tables contain cohort-flow counts, center-level paired comparison estimates and existing resampling limits, and the 32 cells of the criterion-state transition matrices. They contain no patient identifiers, individual time series or credentials. They reproduce the existing study figures; they are not outputs from the fictional synthetic demonstration.

Figure 1 is selection and design; Figure 2 is the prespecified main comparison; Figure 3 is the dated post-result criterion extension; Figure S1 is the prespecified parallel and sensitivity analysis. Refer to METHODS.md and CHANGELOG.md for boundaries. No new patient analysis or resampling is required to redraw these figures.
